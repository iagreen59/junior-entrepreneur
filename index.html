How to play

Quick start

  1. Start with $50. Buy your first stand for $20 before Sell Day unlocks.
  2. Each day, choose which menu items to offer: juice, cocoa, burger, and/or soup. Weather has five levels — from steaming hot to bitter cold — and shifts what sells.

Before you sell

  1. Own a stand (buy one if you haven’t yet; add more when cash > $100).
  2. With 2+ stands, staff every location (you +/or employees).
  3. Check today’s Weather (steaming hot / hot / mild / cold / bitter cold).
  4. Set Today’s menu toggles (at least one item on).
  5. For each offered item: open Recipe or Price and use the item dropdown to switch products (closing or switching without save discards edits). Buy stock into the shared bag, then press Sell Day — customers choose among offered items (~10s); wages are paid then.

Growing business

  1. When cash is over $100, you may Add stand ($20) up to 4. Use the stand dropdown to switch which location you manage; the map shows every stand you own. Inventory stays shared.
  2. With 2+ stands, staff every stand: you may run one yourself; hire employees for the others (or run all with employees). Stand wage is $5/day per employee, paid on Sell Day. Understaffed stands block Sell Day. You may Sell a stand for $10 (keep at least one).
  3. Rare morning events (less than about one per week) show a banner — supply prices up or down, an employee quitting, or a foot-traffic surge. Impacts are temporary and recoverable; never an instant game over.
  4. Open Business for the ledger (Business summary) or a Previous Day report (Daily summary — pick a completed day). Tap (?) on a metric for a short explanation. Use Inventory below Sell Day to peek at stock without cluttering the screen.
  5. Use New Game anytime to reset. Hide these instructions with Hide; Show instructions brings them back.

What customers like

Match your menu to the weather and tune each recipe so buyers react with happy faces instead of walk-aways.

  • Juice — extra ice on steaming hot and hot days; a fruit-forward blend on mild days.
  • Hot cocoa — rich, chocolaty flavor (more chocolate, whipped cream, and sprinkles) on cold and bitter-cold days.
  • Burger — loaded with lettuce, tomato, and cheese on hot and steaming days when grill food is in demand.
  • Soup — a hearty bowl with extra broth, noodles, and veggies on cold and bitter-cold days.

Weather guide: steaming hot & hot favor juice and burgers; cold & bitter cold favor cocoa and soup; mild days treat all four about the same. After Sell Day, tap Show hints on the day-results panel for recipe tips from that day.

Restaurants

  1. Own 4 stands with cash over $1000 to buy a restaurant for $400 (all stands are forfeited — you cannot own stands and restaurants together). Hire 2–4 employees per restaurant; you cannot work a restaurant shift. Sell Day charges rent $18/day per restaurant plus $8/day per employee — overhead is a profitability challenge, so watch each location’s P&L.
  2. Cash over $1000 unlocks more restaurants (max 4), each with its own staff and rent. Sell a restaurant for $200 (keep at least one). Selling the last restaurant returns you to stand mode with one stand — a viable choice if rent and wages eat your profit.
